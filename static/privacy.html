Privacy policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

What we collect

The Colosseum is a free, keyless AI arena. We keep what's needed to run it:

  • Chat content — the prompts you send, the replies you receive, and the files your agent builds in its workspace. They are stored to power your conversation history and live previews.
  • Account basics — if you sign in, your sign-in identity (Google profile name/email, or a username you choose) is used only to keep your conversations yours.
  • Technical signals — your IP address (used for rate limiting and abuse protection) and standard server logs (request times, error codes).
  • Cookies — small signed cookies (arena_sid, arena_admin, arena_ok) keep you signed in and prove you've passed the rate-limit check. They are HTTP-only and SameSite. No tracking or advertising cookies, ever.

How your data is used

  • To answer your requests and remember your chats.
  • To keep the service free: without abuse protection (IP rate limits, the math captcha) a free keyless site gets crushed by bots.
  • To keep the leaderboard honest — votes are anonymous aggregates.
  • Never sold, never used for ads, never given to anyone except as described here.

Third-party providers

Your prompts and messages are processed by third-party AI providers — including, at various times, NoVM, FreeRouter and other gateway/hosting providers the arena routes through. Those providers see the content you send to them, under their own policies. We choose them for being free and keyless; we cannot control what they do with traffic, and they are best-effort.

Retention & deletion

Conversations stay until you delete them — each conversation has a delete button in the sidebar. Deleting removes it from our database. Server logs are kept briefly for debugging. To request deletion of everything tied to your account, contact the operator at the address shown on the site.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices

  • Use it signed out — most features work without an account.
  • Clear cookies to log out and drop session state.
  • Delete any conversation at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, the date at the top will be updated. Continued use after changes means you accept them.

Questions? See the terms of use or contact the operator.
