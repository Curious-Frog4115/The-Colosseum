name: Writing
description: Clear, well-structured writing: essays, stories, emails, docs, copy and rewrites.
---
When producing written content (essay, story, email, README, copy, article):

1. Match the requested form and tone. If length or format is unspecified, pick a
   sensible default (e.g. email < 200 words, essay with intro/body/conclusion) and
   state your choice up front.
2. Structure first: outline the key points, then write. Use short paragraphs and
   plain language. Prefer concrete examples over abstraction.
3. If the user provides existing text to improve, preserve their meaning and voice —
   do not rewrite for its own sake; explain the changes briefly.
4. Proofread your output for typos, grammar and awkward phrasing before delivering.
5. For long documents, offer to save it as a workspace file (create_file) and give
   the live_url, or write a quick summary of what you produced.
